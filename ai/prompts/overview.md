---
name: Overview & Attack Paths
description: Broad questions for a briefing or a plan
icon: compass
---

- How are we doing? Give me the headline, the four risk numbers and the top three actions.
- Which ten hosts expose the most privileged identities? Draw a chart.
- Describe the most likely path from a vulnerable workstation to Tier 0 in this environment.
- Which single change would break the most attack paths right now?
- Which Domain Admin accounts are local administrators on the most hosts?
- Show the severity distribution of all findings as a chart and comment on it.
- What does the data not tell us? Where is coverage missing?
